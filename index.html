Hello world! I am Hailey, and I tell data-driven stories.

I am a data and graphics reporter at the Los Angeles Times . I graduated from Berkeley Graduate School of Journalism .

Previously, I was a reporter at Bloomberg , Lookout Santa Cruz , Oakland North , Rhythms Monthly and Business Today.

I write, code and make maps. When I am not in front of my laptop, I can be found in a cinema or a local bookstore.
